# Quackstreet_Crossing_proj

Imagine you're a duck. You wake up to your alarm ready to head to the store. As you're crossing the street to reach the pond, you get hit by a bus. The next morning, you wake up late and in a fluster, unable to hear your alarm. You realise you're deaf. See what it's like to cross the road and live deaf through the eyes of a duck.
